Season Guide

Pick the atmosphere you want, not just the map.

Clear-sky Season

October to March

Best for balanced travel across hills, backwaters, heritage towns, and beaches.

Sun and Escape Season

April to May

Ideal if you want quieter stays, hill station weather, and slower resort days.

Monsoon Season

June to September

Perfect for rain lovers, lush landscapes, Ayurvedic stays, and cinematic scenery.
